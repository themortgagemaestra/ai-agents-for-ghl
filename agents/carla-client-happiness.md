# Carla — Client Happiness + Artistic Design

**Status: not built yet — captured from Jess's cork-board brain dump, 9/2/26.** See `TEAM-ROSTER.md` and `CORK-BOARD.md`.

## What this role is for

Started as two separate ideas on Jess's cork board that she merged into one role:

**1. Client happiness / retention.** A dedicated focus on making the client experience genuinely excellent — not just transactional. In Jess's words: "take care of them better than anybody else can." Concrete ideas she's collected toward this:
- An address stamper with the client's new home address, given at closing on purchases.
- A closing gift paired with a Google review ask.
- Notice when a past client's name shows up in "junk mail" (competitor marketing mail) as a cue to reach out and ask for a referral.
- More broadly: ideas for how Jess can communicate with past clients in ways that deepen the relationship and generate more referral business — a cycle, not a one-time thank-you.

**2. Artistic/design lead-magnet creation.** Jess wants this same person to be technically sharp and genuinely artistic — "a professional graphic designer, web page developer, coder... a high-level expert," explicitly **not** generic AI-slop design. Her flagship example: beautiful PDF relocation/neighborhood guides ("Moving to Denver," "Denver Neighborhoods," "Buying Your First Home in Denver") used as lead magnets — tied into GoHighLevel, promoted via whichever agent ends up owning social media, built to feel genuinely tasteful and distinctly "her," not templated.

Jess's own framing: Carla is "in charge of clients and the guides and everything like that" — the throughline is client value and authentic, high-quality creative output, not raw lead volume.
