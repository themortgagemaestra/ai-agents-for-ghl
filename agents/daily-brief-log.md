# Daily Brief Log — Gloria

The shared running log for Gloria's two daily touchpoints — morning check-in and evening wrap-up. Newest entry at top, same pattern as `EXECUTION-LEDGER.md`. Originally split across two separate agents (Bridgette, morning; Grecia, evening); consolidated into one — Gloria — on 9/2/26 at Jess's request, since she wanted one consistent voice across the whole day rather than two. The evening half still reads the morning half, and vice versa the next day, exactly as before — that mechanic didn't change, just who's speaking.

Modeled on a real "chief of staff" morning-brief demo from Amber McCue's AI Clone Team training, then stripped down hard per Jess's direction (9/2/26): plain, direct, eighth-grade-simple language, no decorative filler (no energy/astrology cards, no vague "ratio check" paragraphs, no hedged "could not verify" padding). If a line needs a paragraph to explain why it matters, it doesn't belong here.

## Entry template

```
### YYYY-MM-DD

**Gloria — Morning**
- One Big Thing: [the single headline task]
- On deck (after the Big Thing): [short list, 2-3 items]
- Gratitude reminder: sent (Jess's own physical journal — not captured here)
- Social spark: [one line]
- Market/loan update: [one line, dated]
- Carried over from last night: [pulled from yesterday's evening entry, if anything]

**Gloria — Evening**
- Accomplishment inspo: [candidate list of things Gloria knows Jess got done today, sourced from this repo's own git activity (GHL activity once connected) — not a fixed count, not Jess's actual journal entry, just raw material for her to pick from when she writes her own physical journal, celebrated warmly and specifically]
- Didn't get to: [short list]
- Tomorrow's Big Thing: [carried into tomorrow morning's entry]
```

No real entries yet — this file is ready to use now that Gloria's routines are live.

## Note on the evening "wins" entry (resolved 9/2/26, still applies post-consolidation)

Gloria doesn't write Jess's journal for her and doesn't need to land on exactly 6 items. She surfaces whatever she genuinely noticed Jess get done that day as raw inspiration — Jess reads it and decides what actually goes in her physical journal. The log holds Gloria's candidate list, not Jess's real journal entry.
