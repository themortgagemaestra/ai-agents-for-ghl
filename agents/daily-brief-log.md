# Daily Brief Log — Gloria

The shared running log for Gloria's two daily touchpoints — morning check-in and evening wrap-up. Newest entry at top, same pattern as `EXECUTION-LEDGER.md`. Originally split across two separate agents (Bridgette, morning; Grecia, evening); consolidated into one — Gloria — on 9/2/26 at Jess's request, since she wanted one consistent voice across the whole day rather than two. The evening half still reads the morning half, and vice versa the next day, exactly as before — that mechanic didn't change, just who's speaking.

Modeled on a real "chief of staff" morning-brief demo from Amber McCue's AI Clone Team training, then stripped down hard per Jess's direction (9/2/26): plain, direct, eighth-grade-simple language, no decorative filler (no energy/astrology cards, no vague "ratio check" paragraphs, no hedged "could not verify" padding). If a line needs a paragraph to explain why it matters, it doesn't belong here.
